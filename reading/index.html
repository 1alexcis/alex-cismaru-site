Video · Alex Dekora on Cal Newport’s Deep Work

Deep Work

The productivity claims are a bit much, but the advice about protecting blocks of time is useful. A coding problem or a track needs enough uninterrupted time to make progress. What I take from this is to protect that time, then actually stop working.

Book · Brad Gilbert & Steve Jamison

Winning Ugly

Playing tennis makes the distinction pretty clear: the best-looking shot isn’t always the right one. Gilbert’s advice is to pay attention to your opponent and choose a plan you can actually execute. I like that emphasis on decisions, especially when a match gets tight.

Essay · Paul Graham

Do Things that Don’t Scale

For something like Sunny, watching a few people use the app seems more useful than guessing what thousands might want. I like the permission to do things manually early on. It gives you a chance to learn what needs building before you automate it.

Essay · Paul Graham

How to Get Startup Ideas

I’m drawn to projects I’d use myself, especially in music and software. This essay explains why that can be a good starting point. The part worth checking is whether other people actually have the same problem; being your own first user only gets you so far.
